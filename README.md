# The Higher Place, Spinnaker House

Interactive 3D design concept for **The Higher Place** at Spinnaker House, 142 Greenwich High Road, London SE10 8NN.

*Love is not what we do, it is who we are.*

**Live model:** https://adeniyikayodee.github.io/the-higher-place/

- Site footprint measured from Google satellite imagery; heights estimated from Street View (allow about ±0.5 m).
- Three hall layouts: Gathering (theatre rows), Arc (curved rows) and Café Church (round tables).
- Interiors, the mezzanine and the hall's side windows are proposals, not a survey.

Built with three.js. Single file: `index.html`.
